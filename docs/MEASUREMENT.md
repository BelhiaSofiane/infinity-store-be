# Conversion rationale and measurement proposal

The homepage moves from purpose, to a transparent catalog state, to decision steps, answers and an inquiry route. A single dark primary catalog button leads discovery; WhatsApp is a secondary inquiry action. Support stays accessible from navigation, footer and contact. No invented speed claims, prices, scarcity, customer counts or reviews. No analytics is installed.

Proposed measurement only, requiring authorization and privacy assessment:

| Concept | Definition | Limit / denominator |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product discovery | Eligible product detail viewed | Eligible catalog visitors with lawful measurement coverage |
| Product inquiry click | Sales WhatsApp link activated | Product viewers; a click is not a sent message |
| Qualified inquiry | Genuine relevant buying request manually classified by staff | Actual buying conversations, excluding spam and existing-customer service |
| Paid order | Verified accepted transaction with recorded payment | Qualified inquiries from the same defined cohort; not website visitors |
| First human response | Staff response during confirmed Brussels working hours | Real service requests; exclude automated replies |
| Resolution | Actual documented close/outcome | Show unresolved cases, refund/problem rate and complaints alongside sales metrics |

Manual attribution: staff may ask how a customer found the business and record only a coarse source/category in a restricted log. Do not place identities, message contents, order references or keys in tracking URLs/events. Keep inquiry and support classification separate. Unattributed WhatsApp sales remain unattributed. Optional analytics would need documented event definitions, consent/legal basis, retention, coverage limits, and visitor denominators before installation.

Prioritized backlog: (1) approve seller/product/service evidence; (2) test actual compatibility/region explanations with customer questions; (3) improve qualified-inquiry wording based on observed confusion; (4) introduce factual comparison only if the catalog supports it; (5) establish a lawful baseline before controlled experiments. These are hypotheses, with no measured uplift, conversions or statistical significance claimed.
